Upcoming College Events & Activities

Check the schedule of technical, cultural, and sports events organized by City Engineering College:

1. TechNova 2026 - National Level Technical Fest

Date: 14 - 15 November 2026  |  Time: 09:30 AM to 05:00 PM  |  Venue: College Main Auditorium & CSE Department Labs

Description: TechNova is the annual technical festival featuring competitive coding contests, blind programming, web development sprint, tech quiz, and technical paper presentations. Open to all engineering students across departments.

Faculty Coordinator: Dr. K. Raman (Department of CSE)

2. 24-Hour Smart Campus Hackathon

Date: 21 - 22 November 2026  |  Time: Starts 10:00 AM (Continuous 24 Hours)  |  Venue: Central Computing Center, 2nd Floor

Description: Teams of 3 to 4 students collaborate to prototype web and mobile software solutions for campus automation, energy conservation, and student grievance tracking. Mentors from IT companies will evaluate final prototypes.

Faculty Coordinator: Prof. S. Sen (Operating Systems Faculty)

3. Annual Inter-Department Sports Tournament

Date: 05 - 08 December 2026  |  Time: 08:00 AM to 04:30 PM  |  Venue: College Sports Ground & Indoor Gymnasium

Description: Annual athletic and team sports competitions including Cricket, Football, Volleyball, Badminton, Table Tennis, 100m sprint, and Chess. Trophies and medals awarded at the closing ceremony.

Faculty Coordinator: Coach V. Patil (Physical Education Director)

4. Workshop on Cloud Deployment & DevOps

Date: 18 December 2026  |  Time: 10:00 AM to 01:00 PM  |  Venue: Seminar Hall A, Science Block

Description: A practical introductory workshop covering Linux command line essentials, containerization with Docker, Git version control, and cloud server hosting fundamentals for pre-final year students.

Faculty Coordinator: Prof. M. Joshi (Software Engineering Faculty)
