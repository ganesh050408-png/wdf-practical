College Notices & Circulars

Official announcements, examination schedules, administrative instructions, and campus circulars:

Mid-Term Sessional Examination Schedule (Nov 2026)

Notice No: CEC/EXAM/2026/42  |  Date: 20-October-2026

The Mid-Term Sessional Examinations for 4th Semester B.Tech students will commence from Monday, 10th November 2026. The examination will carry 30 marks per theory subject and will be held in the respective department classrooms. Detailed subject-wise timetable is updated on the Timetable page. Admit cards can be collected from the department office starting 05-Nov-2026.

Issued by: Controller of Examinations, City Engineering College

Central Library Clearance and Book Renewal Notice

Notice No: CEC/LIB/2026/18  |  Date: 17-October-2026

All students who have issued textbooks or reference books from the Central Library before 30th September 2026 are advised to return or renew their books on or before 30th October 2026. Overdue fines of Rs. 5 per day will apply to all unrenewed books after the due date.

Issued by: Head Librarian, Central Library

National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Renewal 2026-27

Notice No: CEC/SCH/2026/09  |  Date: 12-October-2026

Students who received government scholarships in their 1st and 2nd academic years must submit their scholarship renewal verification forms along with previous semester mark sheets to the Student Welfare Section (Room 104) before 31st October 2026. Late submissions cannot be uploaded to the central portal.

Issued by: Student Welfare Officer

Semester Tuition Fee Installment Deadline

Notice No: CEC/ACC/2026/33  |  Date: 05-October-2026

The final date for paying the 2nd installment of semester tuition fees without a late fine is 15th November 2026. Payments can be completed online through net banking or via Demand Draft in favour of "City Engineering College" at the accounts counter.

Issued by: Accounts & Finance Department

One-Day Industrial Visit to IT Software Park

Notice No: CEC/CSE/2026/15  |  Date: 28-September-2026

An educational industrial visit for 4th Semester Computer Science students to the State Software Technology Park is scheduled for 25th November 2026. Interested students must submit parental consent forms to Prof. M. Joshi by 10th November 2026. Bus transportation and entry passes are provided by the college.

Issued by: Head of Department, Computer Science & Engineering
